## Application description

Stuff Pretty Good is an AI-assisted commerce publication and email newsletter focused on useful product discovery. The site helps users find practical products, gifts, and starter kits by budget, category, and use case. Promotion methods include editorial buying guides, opt-in email newsletter, AI-powered product recommendation tools, and category-based product roundups. We do not use incentivized clicks, misleading pricing claims, trademark bidding, coupon abuse, or unapproved outbound merchant links. We use clear affiliate disclosures on site and in email.

Do not mention list size. Use this if asked about audience: launching with a staged reactivation of an owned, previously opted-in media audience, using strict suppression handling, preference capture, and one-click unsubscribe.
